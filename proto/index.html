Habit & fall

Shear profile — the hero control

Speed (m/s) at six heights, generating level first. A turning wind is what hooks a fallstreak — that is uncinus rather than fibratus.

Advected detail

Detail rides the flow and is re-seeded at the cells. It is never sampled from noise at the shading point — that is the difference between fibres that flow and fibres that shimmer.

Droplet phase (Jendersie–d'Eon)

The liquid model, kept live so the two can be compared. Ice habits get their own phase library at Phase 4 — that is what produces 22° and 46° haloes, sundogs and pillars, and it is what a spherical-droplet model cannot reach.
